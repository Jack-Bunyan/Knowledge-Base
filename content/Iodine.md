# Biodistribution of Iodine
#CB_Notion 
>“You don’t deserve to be given a certificate to work as a Nuclear Medicine Technologist if you don’t know the biodistribution of Iodine.”
>	\- RC, Chief Nuclear Medicine Technologist, HUTH

## \[P461+ in Requisites\]

Radioiodine uptake can be seen in [[thyroid]], [[salivary glands]], [[stomach]] and choroid plexus. Excretion is via [[Anatomy & Physiology-Kidneys|kidneys]] and [[gastrointestinal tract]] so these will be seen in images.

- Uptake is rapid in the thyroid, usually in 20-30 minutes and continues to increase over 24 hours.

![[IodineUptake-Images.png|697]]
Performing an [[Iodine#I-123 Scan|I-123 Scan]] prior to [[Iodine#I-131 Therapy|I-131 Ablation]] could result in substandard results, as the [[thyroid]] and other iodine avid cells will already be flooded and no longer uptaking more.

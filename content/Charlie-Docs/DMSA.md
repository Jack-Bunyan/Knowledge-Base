- 99mTc-dimercaptosuccinic acid (DMSA) is used in [[Anatomy & Physiology-Kidneys#Kidney Structure|renal cortical]] imaging.
	- it binds to the sulfhydryl groups in the proximal tubule cells
		- with a long effective half-life within the kidneys
			- leading to an increase in [[Dose#Effective Dose]]


![[DMSE uptake mech figure.png]]*Uptake mechanism of 99mTc-DMSA ([Source](https://www.researchgate.net/figure/Mechanism-for-renal-uptake-of-99m-Tc-DMSA-A-a1-microglobulin-bound-99m-Tc-DMSA-is_fig2_233900793))*

- After injection, 90% of the dose is bound to [[On Blood#Plasma|plasma]] proteins ([Source](https://radiopaedia.org/articles/tc-99m-dmsa?lang=gb)).
- ~40% of the injected dose is bound in the renal cortex.
- the remainder is slowly excreted over following hours.
- there is maximum cortical uptake after 3 hours of injection.
# Patient Preparation

- Ask the patient if they have had any recent UTIs (within the last 6 months). If so, record this in the comments field of the RIS.
    - it is ideal to do the test without a current UTI, however in some paediatric cases it can be performed whilst the patient has one
- Ensure the patient is well hydrated before and after administration of the radiopharmaceutical

# Imaging Procedure
Views are static and captured on a LEHR collimator with standard $140keV\pm10\%$ energy window level.

# Extras from Wednesday Funday

DTPA (cheap - £8/vial (16 packs of custard creams)) - mix with water and shake, easier preparation. 1 measure of the kidney

[[Tc99m-MAG3]] (expensive) - boil for 10 minutes and more expensive. Centres like to QC MAG3 as it is being boiled, must use within 4 hours (now an 8 hour life). Must be made from fresh eluate, generator less than a week old, eluate drawn within the last 24 hours. MAG3 is cleared by tubular excretion, with higher renal uptake giving better renal to background images. MAG3 is a lot cheaper now so better to use. tubular secretion and glomuler filtration, 2 measures of the kidney.

Give furosemide, gets dose out of kidney so we know there isn’t a blockage in the kidney.

Furosemide discriminates between an obstructed kidney and a normal one.

[[I-123 Hipuran]] is gold standard for renal function.

# [Referral Criteria Explanation](https://cdn.ymaws.com/www.bnms.org.uk/resource/resmgr/guidelines/renal_cortical_scintigraphy_.pdf)
- Renal parenchymal scarring (at least 4-6 months following an acute UTI)
- Characterisation of structural renal abnormalities: e.g. solitary, duplex, small, dysplastic, horseshoe, and psuedo-horseshoe kidney
- Detection of ectopic renal tissue, including cross-fused renal ectopia
- Differential renal function
- Pre-transplant donor assessment

Collimators have a vital role in ionising radiation imaging.  They are necessary to reduce the acceptance angle of the incoming radiation in order to increase the [[spatial resolution]] of the collected images.  They are used in [[CT]], [[Nuclear medicine]], and [[Planar X-ray]].
